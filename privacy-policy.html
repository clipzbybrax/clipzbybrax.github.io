Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Summary

Clipz by Brax ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner, Braxton Landry, to upload the owner's own video clips to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. It does not collect data from anyone else.

What data the app uses

When the owner connects a TikTok account, TikTok provides the app with:

  • Basic profile information (an account ID and display name), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens that let the app upload videos to that account, as approved by the owner.

The app also uses the owner's own video files and the captions and titles the owner writes.

Where data is stored

All of this stays on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored in a local file that is not uploaded or shared. The app has no server and no database outside the owner's computer.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share data with anyone. Videos and captions are sent only to the platform the owner chooses to post to (for example TikTok), through that platform's official API.

Other people's data

The app does not collect, store or process data about any other TikTok users.

Removing access and data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (or Manage app permissions), and by deleting the local token file. Deleting it removes all stored TikTok data from the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
