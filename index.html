Clipz by Brax

Clipz by Brax is a personal desktop tool. It turns long videos into short vertical clips with captions on the owner's own computer, and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts through each platform's official API.

It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.
