What this app is

Clipz by Brax ("the app") is a personal desktop tool run by its owner, Braxton Landry. It edits video clips on the owner's own computer and uploads them to the owner's own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Who may use it

Only the owner uses the app, with accounts the owner controls. No one else can sign up for or use the app.

Content

The owner only uploads content they have the right to post (their own videos, content from creators who have given permission, or properly licensed material) and follows each platform's terms and community guidelines, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Platform connections

The app connects to TikTok only through TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit and the Content Posting API) after the account owner signs in and approves access. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (or Manage app permissions).
